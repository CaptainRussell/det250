    How to wear it

    • Order. Highest award is on the top row at the wearer's right (your left when you look at the rack). The top row holds the leftovers and is centered. AFROTC Sup 10.8.1.1, Fig 10.4
    • Prior service ribbons earned on active duty go ahead of all AFROTC ribbons. AFROTC Sup 10.8.1.8 JROTC ribbons are not authorized on the AFROTC uniform. Only awards in AFROTCI 36-2011V3 may be worn. AFROTCI 36-2011V3 15.3.2
    • Civil Air Patrol: wear only your highest CAP award. Fig 10.4
    • Service dress coat: wear all or some ribbons, centered and resting on (but not over) the edge of the pocket on your left. The bottom of the name tag lines up with the bottom of the ribbons. DAFI 36-2903 4.5.2.2, 4.5.2.3
    • Blue shirt or blouse: ribbons are optional. If worn, center them on your left, parallel with the ground, with the bottom of the ribbons even with the bottom of the name tag. DAFI 36-2903 4.7.3
    • Rows and the short top row: the coat lapel may cover part of the rack. Figure 13.1 shows racks where the lapel does not cover the ribbons (or on other uniforms) with the short top row centered, and racks where the lapel covers part of them with the short rows kept to the outside, away from the lapel. Other arrangements are authorized as needed. No space between rows. DAFI 36-2903 13.1.2, 13.5.2
    • Devices: a bronze oak leaf cluster for each added award (each completed scholarship year on the College Scholarship ribbon); a silver cluster replaces five bronze. Silver devices go to the wearer's right of bronze. AFROTC Sup 10.8.1.5, 10.8.1.6; DAFI 36-2903 13.6
    • Stars: silver star on the FT Distinguished Graduate ribbon, the national winner of the Outstanding CTA award, and the AFA Outstanding Cadet of the Year. Bronze star on the two weapon Expert Marksmanship ribbon and the AFA Lovelace award. AFROTC Sup 10.8.1.3, 10.8.1.4
    • Fitness Award: earned with a 95 or higher on the PFA, once per term. A first 100 adds one silver star, worn centered or to the wearer's right of any oak leaf clusters. AFROTCI 36-2011V3 Table 15.1
    • FT Distinguished Graduate and Superior Performer: both use the same ribbon. The DG wears it with a silver star; the Superior Performer wears it without a device, right after the DG. NOTACC CY25-119; AFROTC Sup 10.8.1.4
    • Organizational awards (Legion of Valor, American Legion, ROA, MOWW and others) wear only one device, centered, even if earned more than once. AFROTC Sup 10.8.1.7
    • Badges: your first badge is centered 1/2 inch above the top row of ribbons. DAFI 36-2903 12.2.4
