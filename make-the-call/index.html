Rules card
  • Every cadet picks Agree, Neutral, or Disagree on their own. Your team doesn't have to agree.
  • The pool is fixed. Everyone in the winning position splits it; each share goes to that cadet's team. Smaller winning group means bigger shares.
  • Neutral must make a case: name the missing information or condition that would move you to Agree or Disagree.
  • Board members score independently first, then name the decisive argument out loud.
  • Rotate speakers. No one speaks twice until everyone in the group has spoken once.
  • Debrief: What did this show about effective communication or commander's intent?
